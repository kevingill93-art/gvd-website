Fan Content Policy

God vs the Devil is an open universe.

If these characters make you want to draw, write, film, perform, remix, or build something of your own, do it. That is what they are for. Fan art, fan fiction, fan videos, cosplay, streams, and whole fan-made corners of this world are welcome here, and they always will be.

A few simple terms keep it fair for everyone:

  1. Give credit. Say the work comes from God vs the Devil and link back to godvsthedevil.com or the official God vs the Devil YouTube channel. One line is enough.
  2. Do not sell it. Fan work stays free. No charging for it, no monetizing it, no merchandise, unless you have a written license from us first.
  3. Do not claim it. The universe and its characters are original creations. Enjoy them, build with them, and leave the authorship where it belongs. Never present fan work as the official product.
  4. Keep it in the spirit of the show. GVD is a 13+ comedy. Fan work under this policy stays 13+: no porn, no hate content, and no campaigning or agendas hung on these characters.

Everything official (the canon, the episodes, the story) lives at godvsthedevil.com. Everything you make lives wherever you make it, and we are glad it exists. If we love something you made, we may share it, with credit to you.

One more thing, because it is how this show itself was built: GVD wears its inspirations openly, and some of its characters are loving mashups of things the creator grew up on. Do the same with ours. Blend these characters into new creations of your own, hide Easter eggs, make the composite only you would make. Under the same terms above, that is not a loophole. That is the tradition.

This policy may grow as the universe does. The welcome will not shrink.

Punch up. Never down. Have fun.
